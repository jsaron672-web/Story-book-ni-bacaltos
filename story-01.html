by J.K. Rowling

Fantasy

Synopsis

A young boy discovers that he is a wizard and begins studying magic at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

Chapter 1: The Letter on the Doormat

Mina found the envelope wedged under the bakery door, addressed in green ink to 'The Girl Who Fixes Clocks.' She almost threw it away. Then the wax seal warmed in her hand and the clock above the counter began ticking backwards.

By nightfall a tall stranger with a lantern was knocking. 'Pack light,' he said. 'The school does not like latecomers, and the staircases do not wait.' Mina grabbed her tools and stepped into the dark.

The stranger introduced himself as Mr. Alder and led her to a train platform that was not on any map. A scarlet engine hissed there, steaming with purple smoke. Mina climbed aboard with her heart racing.

Inside, a boy with a toad and a girl with a stack of books argued over a seat. 'Are you a first-year too?' the girl asked. Mina nodded, and for the first time all year, she did not feel alone.

The school stood on a cliff above a black lake, its windows glowing like a thousand patient eyes. The first-years crossed in little boats while a stern headmistress called out names. Mina gripped the rail and felt every clock in her pocket tick faster.

In the Great Hall, a tall door rose before them. 'Step inside,' said the headmistress. 'The Hall will decide where you belong.' Mina walked in, and every clock on every wall began chiming together. Someone gasped. Someone laughed. Mina, at last, felt at home.
