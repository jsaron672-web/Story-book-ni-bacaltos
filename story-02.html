The Hunger Games

The Hunger Games

by Suzanne Collins

Dystopian / Science Fiction

Synopsis

Katniss Everdeen volunteers to participate in a dangerous televised competition to save her younger sister.

Chapter 1: Reaping Day

The siren sounded at noon and the whole district stood in silence. Rue's name was not called, but Ana's heart beat as if it had been. Her little brother squeezed her sleeve and did not let go.

When the name finally rang out, it was the wrong one. Ana heard her own voice answer before she could think. 'I volunteer.' Every head turned, and the cameras turned with them.

The train to the Capitol was clean, silent and too fast. Ana pressed her forehead against the glass and watched her district shrink into a smudge of gray. Across the aisle, a boy her age stared at his hands.

'I'm Jory,' he said at last. 'We'll be training together.' Ana studied him carefully. Everyone had told her never to trust the other tributes, but nobody had said how lonely that rule would feel.

The Capitol rose out of the mist like a city made of glass and sugar. Crowds roared as the train rolled in, throwing flowers that landed on the windows. Ana forced a smile because Jory nudged her and hissed, 'Wave. Everyone is watching.'

That night, a tired mentor with a scar on her chin sat beside her. 'Smiles keep you alive out here,' she said, 'but only if you know which ones are real.' Ana touched the little pin on her collar and whispered her sister's name. Then she promised herself, again, that she was coming home.
