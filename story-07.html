The Little Prince

The Little Prince

by Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

Fantasy / Philosophical Fiction

Synopsis

A young prince travels between planets and discovers lessons about friendship, love, responsibility, and life.

Chapter 1: The Boy from Asteroid B

The pilot's engine had died, and so had his patience. Then a small voice behind him said, 'Please, draw me a sheep.' He turned and found a golden-haired boy standing in the sand.

'You are not from here,' said the pilot. 'Nobody is from anywhere,' the boy replied seriously. 'But I have a flower back home who is waiting for me.' The desert went very quiet.

The pilot gave him a drawing of a box and told him the sheep was inside. The boy's face lit up. 'This is exactly the sheep I wanted!' he said, and fell asleep beside it as though nothing could be more natural.

All night the pilot sat awake, watching the stars. For the first time in years, he wondered how many of them had flowers growing on them, and whether he had ever thought to ask.

In the morning, the boy told the pilot about his tiny planet: three small volcanoes, a patch of grass and one proud flower with four thorns. She coughed whenever she wanted attention and complained about drafts. 'She was impossible,' he said, 'and I loved her.'

'Why did you leave?' the pilot asked. 'I was too young to know how to love her,' the boy said. 'But now I think I understand.' He looked at the horizon, where the first sun was turning the sand to gold. 'First, I want to see everything. Then I will go home.'

End of Chapter 1
