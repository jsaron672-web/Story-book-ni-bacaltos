The Maze Runner

The Maze Runner

by James Dashner

Science Fiction / Thriller

Synopsis

A teenager wakes up in a mysterious enclosed environment and works with other boys to solve the secrets of a dangerous maze.

Chapter 1: The Box Goes Up

Theo woke in a rattling metal box, remembering nothing but his first name. When the lid opened, a ring of strangers peered down at him and one of them said, 'Another greenie.'

They led him through a field of tall stone walls that shifted every night. 'Nobody goes in after sundown,' said the boy beside him. 'The ones who tried never came back to tell us why.'

At dawn the doors in the stone wall slid open and the Runners dashed into the maze. Theo watched them vanish into the shadows, and asked the leader what they were looking for. 'A way out,' he said simply.

'For how long?' Theo asked. The leader did not answer right away. 'Two years,' he said at last. Theo stared at the walls, and something strange stirred in his memory, a hallway, a number, a whisper.

By evening, the Runners returned, all but one. The boys watched the gap in the wall. When the great doors began to close, grinding like old teeth, the leader raised a hand. 'No one goes after him,' he said. His voice cracked. 'We wait.'

Theo could not sleep. He climbed the watchtower and stared into the dark corridors. Something small and metallic crawled along a wall, a mechanical beetle with a number on its back. It was his number, the same one in his head. Its red eye blinked at him once, as if it knew he had finally remembered.

End of Chapter 1
