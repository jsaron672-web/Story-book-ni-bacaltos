The Fault in Our Stars

The Fault in Our Stars

by John Green

Romance / Young Adult

Synopsis

Two teenagers meet at a cancer support group and develop a meaningful relationship while facing difficult circumstances.

Chapter 1: The Support Group

The chairs were arranged in a circle, which Lia thought was a cruel joke for people who already felt cornered. She sat near the cookies and decided to say nothing at all.

Then a boy across the room caught her eye and mouthed, 'Worst snacks ever.' She laughed out loud, and the leader looked up, delighted. It was the first time in months she had forgotten to be sick.

Her name was Hazel, and she wheeled a small oxygen tank behind her like a loyal pet. Across the circle, the boy was named Gus, and he held an unlit cigarette between his fingers. 'It's a metaphor,' he said. 'You put the thing that kills you right between your teeth, but you never give it the power to do it.'

Lia rolled her eyes, but she was smiling. After the meeting, they sat on the church steps and argued about a book until the streetlights came on. For once, the future did not feel like the only subject.

After the meeting, Lia and Gus walked to the bus stop and argued about everything: books, movies, and whether the cookies were secretly made of cardboard. He walked her all the way home, though it was not on his way.

Her mother asked how the group went. 'Not terrible,' Lia said, and went upstairs. Her phone buzzed on the bed. 'Same time next Thursday? Bring better snacks.' She read it three times, hugged a pillow and found the hum of her oxygen tank sounding almost like a song.
