How to Win Friends and Influence People

How to Win Friends and Influence People

by Dale Carnegie

Self-Help / Communication

Synopsis

This book presents practical principles for improving communication, building relationships, and working effectively with others.

Chapter 1: The Name on the Badge

At the networking event, Paolo tried to impress everyone and impressed no one. People nodded politely and drifted toward the buffet.

Then he tried something different. He asked a stranger a real question and listened to the whole answer. He remembered her name afterwards. She remembered his, and she remembered how it felt to be heard.

At the next gathering, Paolo asked a quiet engineer about his daughter's piano recital. The man's face softened and he talked for ten minutes without stopping. Paolo did not interrupt once.

On the way out, the engineer gripped his hand. 'You're the first person who asked,' he said. Paolo smiled. He had not given a single speech or told a single clever story, and yet he had never been so well liked.

The next day, Paolo had to meet a difficult client who had a reputation for chewing through contractors. He began with something unexpected: 'Thank you for being honest in your last email. It helped us a lot.' The client blinked, then softened.

That evening, Paolo wrote three lines in his notebook: listen first, remember names, praise honestly. He realized that charm had never been the point. Interest was. He closed the book and went to call his mother, whom he had not really listened to in months.
