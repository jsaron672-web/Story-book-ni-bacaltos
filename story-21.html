The Da Vinci Code

The Da Vinci Code

by Dan Brown

Mystery / Thriller

Synopsis

A symbologist investigates a murder and follows a trail of historical clues, secret societies, and hidden messages.

Chapter 1: The Curator's Last Message

The museum curator had arranged his own body beneath the great painting, one arm outstretched, a string of numbers scrawled in ink beside him.

Professor Reyes knelt and stared. 'It isn't a dying message,' she said slowly. 'It's an invitation.' Behind her, the security alarm began to wail.

Reyes and the young detective, Okoye, slipped through the museum's back corridor. The numbers on the floor turned out to be a coordinate, and beneath the painting a small brass plate read, 'Look where the eyes cannot.'

They ran their flashlights over the canvas and found an almost invisible latch behind its frame. A narrow stairway spiraled down into darkness. 'After you,' Okoye said, and Reyes raised an eyebrow. 'Cowards first,' she said, and stepped in.

The stairway ended in a vault of mirrored panels. At its center stood a desk with a half-finished letter, a cipher wheel and a candle that was still warm. Reyes ran a finger over the paper. The ink was barely dry.

Above them, a door slammed. Footsteps crossed the museum floor, slow and heavy. Okoye switched off the flashlight and whispered, 'Whoever invited you here wants you to find this, but not alone.' In the dark, the mirrors reflected a hundred versions of the two of them, and none of them were smiling.

End of Chapter 1
