Dracula

by Bram Stoker

Gothic Horror

Synopsis

A group of people confronts Count Dracula, a mysterious nobleman with a terrifying supernatural secret.

Chapter 1: The Castle Road

The coachman refused to drive any further, crossing himself as the wolves began to sing. Jonathan climbed down with his bag and watched the lights vanish behind him.

A tall figure waited in the castle door with a lamp. 'Welcome,' said the Count. 'Enter freely, and leave something of the happiness you bring.' Jonathan noticed that the host cast no shadow.

The dinner was strange and silent. The Count served no food and drank no wine, and Jonathan's questions about the castle's old library were met with slow, careful smiles. Wolves howled far below the window.

When Jonathan finally excused himself, the Count bowed. 'Sleep well,' he said. 'And do not wander at night. The castle has many rooms, and not all of them are friendly.' Jonathan locked his door and listened to a faint scratching on the other side.

In the morning, Jonathan's door was unlocked from the outside and the Count was nowhere in sight. He wandered the dusty halls and found a room full of maps of London and letters in a dozen hands. Each was addressed to the same stranger. He felt cold.

In the courtyard, a silver mirror hung on a nail. Jonathan raised it and saw only the empty hall behind him. He ran back inside and wrote in his diary, 'I must leave before dusk.' But when he reached the gate, it was locked, the coach had vanished and the sun was already sinking behind the mountains.
