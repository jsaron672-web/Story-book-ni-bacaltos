The Alchemist

The Alchemist

by Paulo Coelho

Adventure / Philosophical Fiction

Synopsis

A shepherd travels in search of treasure and learns about following his dreams and discovering his purpose.

Chapter 1: The Dream Under the Tree

Santi slept beneath the old sycamore and dreamed of a hidden chest buried near a distant pyramid. He dreamed it again the next night, and the night after that.

The old woman in the market charged him a coin and said, 'Dreams repeat when they are tired of being ignored.' Santi sold half his sheep and bought a ticket south.

He walked past fields and rivers, learning that the road is longer than any map suggests. In a crowded square, a thief took the last of his money, and Santi laughed so hard that a nearby merchant offered him a job.

'Why are you smiling?' the merchant asked. 'Because I have lost my savings,' Santi said, 'and I have never felt so free.' That night he slept under an unfamiliar sky and the dream returned, brighter than before.

In the port city, Santi met a lamp merchant who sold compasses that pointed to nothing. 'A map is only paper until someone walks it,' the old man said. He pressed two stones into Santi's palm, one white and one black. 'Use them if you are afraid to choose.'

That night Santi sat by the harbor, tossing the stones from hand to hand. Each time, he felt a small pang of disappointment at the answer. At last he laughed and slid them into his pocket. If a coin toss could make him sad, then he already knew what he wanted.

End of Chapter 1
