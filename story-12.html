The Chronicles of Narnia: The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe

The Chronicles of Narnia: The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe

by C.S. Lewis

Fantasy / Adventure

Synopsis

Four siblings enter a magical world and help fight against a powerful witch who has trapped the land in eternal winter.

Chapter 1: Behind the Fur Coats

Rainy days at Aunt Greta's house meant hide-and-seek, and Lucy's favorite spot was the great wardrobe in the spare room. She pushed past the coats and felt something crunch under her shoes.

It was snow. A lamppost glowed between the pine trees, and a faun with a red scarf stared at her. 'A daughter of Eve,' he whispered. 'Quick, before the Witch notices.'

The faun, whose name was Tumnus, led her to a small house under the hill. There was tea, toast and a fire, and for a few minutes everything felt safe. Then he began to cry, and Lucy asked why.

'I was told to capture any human I met,' he sobbed, 'and hand them to the White Witch. But you are far too kind.' Lucy squeezed his hand. 'I won't let her find you,' she said, though she had no idea how she would keep that promise.

Tumnus led Lucy back to the lamppost, where the snow gave way to a floor of coats. She tumbled out of the wardrobe, breathless, only to discover that no time had passed at all. Her siblings stared. 'Narnia?' her brother Edmund snorted. 'You were gone for half a minute.'

The next afternoon, Lucy tiptoed back to the wardrobe to prove it. She did not notice Edmund following with a smirk, nor the soft whisper of snow under his shoes. Behind them, a cold wind curled through the pines, and the lamppost flickered as if it knew who was coming.
