by Stephen R. Covey

Self-Help / Leadership

Synopsis

A guide to developing personal effectiveness through principles such as responsibility, prioritization, and cooperation.

Chapter 1: Between Stimulus and Response

Dana's manager snapped at her in a meeting, and Dana felt the old heat rise. Usually she snapped back. This time she noticed the gap between what happened and what she would do next.

'I can choose,' she realized. She took a breath, answered calmly and watched the whole room relax. Small gaps can hold very large changes.

That afternoon, Dana made a list of the things she truly wanted: a calmer mind, a career she respected, time for her family. Then she looked at her calendar, which was filled with things that did not belong on that list.

She crossed out four meetings and wrote 'Think' in the space. It felt strange and slightly rebellious. But when the week ended, she had finished a project she had been dreaming about for a year.

At lunch, a mentor named Mr. Ibarra asked her a strange question. 'What do you want people to say at your retirement party?' Dana laughed, then went quiet. She grabbed a napkin and began to write, and the words came faster than she expected.

Back at her desk, she opened her calendar and put the important things in first: family dinner, the project, a walk each morning. The small tasks could fit around them, like sand around stones. It was not magic, but by Friday her week felt like her own.
