Divergent

Divergent

by Veronica Roth

Dystopian / Science Fiction

Synopsis

In a society divided into factions based on personality traits, a young woman discovers that she does not fit into just one group.

Chapter 1: Choosing Day

Five bowls sat on the table: water, earth, fire, glass and stone. Kira was supposed to bleed into one and belong to it forever.

Her test had returned a result that no one would say aloud. She held the knife over the bowls, and for the first time in sixteen years, her family could not tell what she would do.

Kira's family waited in the front row, her brother's jaw tight and her mother pretending not to worry. She knew the rules: whichever bowl you chose, you could never go back. You must give the faction your whole life.

She looked at the glass bowl for honesty, the fire bowl for courage and the water bowl for peace. Then she closed her eyes and let the blade slip across her palm. Blood hissed on hot coals, and the whole room gasped.

The proctor read the result aloud and the hall went quiet. 'Fire,' she said, but her voice shook, because that was only part of the answer. Kira's mother pressed her lips together. Her brother stared at the floor. Kira turned and walked out with the new faction, and her mother mouthed one word: go.

On the train platform, the others sprinted and jumped. A boy held out his hand. 'Trust me,' he said. Kira leapt, landed rolling on a rooftop and laughed, shaking with fear and joy. Whatever she was, she was no longer afraid of choosing.
