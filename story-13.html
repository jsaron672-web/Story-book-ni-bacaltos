by Rick Riordan

Fantasy / Mythology

Synopsis

Percy Jackson discovers that he is a demigod and embarks on a quest involving Greek gods and a stolen lightning bolt.

Chapter 1: My Substitute Is a Monster

On the museum field trip, Leo's substitute teacher stared at him a little too long. Her eyes flickered like a stovetop, and her nails looked suspiciously like claws.

'Give it back, boy,' she hissed. Leo had no idea what 'it' was. But his sword-shaped pen, which he swore he had never owned, felt very real in his hand.

Leo's friend Gabe knew the truth before Leo did. 'You never get hurt,' he whispered on the bus. 'Monsters never bite you. And you can breathe underwater, you freak.' Leo had thought those things were normal.

'Grab your bag,' Gabe said. 'My real name is Grover, and we have maybe two minutes before she comes back.' Leo looked out the window at the sunlit museum and saw something with wings circling overhead.

The monster crashed through the museum doors with a shriek, upsetting a row of statues. Leo stumbled backward and his pen grew heavy in his hand, stretching into a bronze sword that hummed like a tuning fork. He swung without thinking.

The creature burst into golden dust, and the museum went silent. Grover grabbed him and dragged him to a waiting taxi. 'There's a camp for kids like you,' he panted. 'It's three hours away and the driver is not a normal driver. Don't eat anything he offers.' Leo stared at the sword and wondered who he really was.
