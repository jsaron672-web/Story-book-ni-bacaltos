by Robert T. Kiyosaki

Personal Finance / Education

Synopsis

The author shares lessons about financial literacy, assets, liabilities, and different approaches to managing money.

Chapter 1: Two Ways to Think About Money

Two uncles gave Jun two very different pieces of advice. 'Work hard and save,' said the first. 'Make your money work for you,' said the second.

Jun drew two columns on a napkin, one for things that put money in his pocket and one for things that took it out. It was the first financial lesson that made sense.

Jun's first investment was small: a used bicycle that he rented out to friends on weekends. It earned him twenty dollars. It was not much, but he had earned it while sleeping, and that thought hit him hard.

'That is the difference,' his second uncle said. 'The poor trade hours for money. The wise trade ideas and time.' Jun wrote it on the napkin and tucked it into his notebook, where it stayed for years.

Jun tracked every peso for a month. He discovered he was spending a surprising amount on subscriptions he had forgotten and snacks he did not even enjoy. He cancelled the subscriptions and put the savings into a small fund.

His second uncle nodded when he heard. 'Your first asset is not the bicycle,' he said. 'It is the habit of noticing.' Jun smiled and drew a third column on the napkin, labeled 'Things I am learning.' It was already the longest list.
