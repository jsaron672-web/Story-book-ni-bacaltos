Atomic Habits

Atomic Habits

by James Clear

Self-Help / Personal Development

Synopsis

This book explains how small, consistent changes in daily routines can help people develop better habits and improve over time.

Chapter 1: One Percent Better

Marco wanted to run a marathon and could barely finish one block. He hung a motivational poster and made a grand plan. By Friday, the poster was the only thing still standing.

Then he tried something silly: just put on his shoes each morning. Nothing more. By week three, the shoes were taking him outside, one tiny step at a time.

The next morning, he ran for two minutes. Just two. He did not feel like a hero, but he did feel something: a quiet click, like a puzzle piece settling into place. The next day he did it again.

By the end of the month, he was running ten minutes without stopping. 'I'm not changing everything,' he told his sister. 'I'm just changing a little, every day.' She laughed, because that was exactly how a mountain gets climbed.

Marco marked every morning run on a paper calendar. On the eighth day, he overslept and felt the old voice whisper that he had ruined everything. Instead, he laced his shoes that evening. 'Never miss twice,' he told himself.

His sister watched him stretch on the porch. 'I never thought you would last,' she admitted. Marco smiled. The marathon was still a year away, and he was nowhere near ready. But the next ten minutes were enough, and that was exactly the point.
