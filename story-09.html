1984

1984

by George Orwell

Dystopian / Political Fiction

Synopsis

A man struggles to maintain his individuality in a society controlled by an oppressive government that monitors its citizens.

Chapter 1: The Telescreen Hum

The screen on Wren's wall never switched off. It hummed through breakfast, praised the Leader at noon, and watched him fold his clothes at night.

He bought a small notebook from a junk shop, a forbidden thing. His pen shook as he wrote the first line: 'Today I thought a thought that was mine.'

At the Ministry, Wren's job was to rewrite old newspapers so that yesterday's facts agreed with today's. He was good at it, and that frightened him more than anything. He could change a whole year with a single pencil stroke.

After work, he walked home past posters of a watching face. 'Someone will read this notebook someday,' he thought. 'And if not, at least one honest sentence will have existed in the world.' He slipped the book under a loose floorboard.

At the Ministry, a quiet woman named Sela slid a folded slip across his desk. 'Do you also hear the hum stop at night?' it said in pencil. Wren's heart hammered. He crumpled it into his palm and, with great calm, swallowed it before anyone could see.

When the telescreen announced the daily Two Minutes of Cheer, he clapped with everyone, louder than anyone. Across the room, Sela clapped too, her eyes fixed forward. From that day on, every smile Wren wore was a mask he had to remember to put on, and a secret he was proud to keep.

End of Chapter 1
