The Shining

The Shining

by Stephen King

Horror / Psychological Thriller

Synopsis

A family staying at an isolated hotel experiences increasingly disturbing supernatural events.

Chapter 1: Closing Day

The caretaker handed over a ring of keys and a warning. 'Snow blocks the road by November. The hotel gets lonely, and it gets hungry.' Dan's father laughed. Dan did not.

That night, from the empty ballroom, came the sound of glasses clinking and distant applause. Dan pulled the blanket over his head and kept very, very still.

The hotel was grand and empty, with red carpets and a long, silent hallway. Dan's little sister, Tess, tugged his sleeve in the lobby. 'The twins in the corridor said they want to play,' she whispered. 'What twins?' he asked.

Their father said it was only the wind in the vents. But that night, Dan heard a tricycle rattling past his door. He did not look out. He only prayed morning would come before the thing in the corridor got bored of waiting.

Morning was cheerful. Dan's father whistled as he set up a typewriter in the lounge and Tess drew two girls in blue dresses on the fridge door. 'They told me their names,' she said. Dan took the drawing and folded it into his pocket without looking.

That night, he woke to the clack of keys downstairs, endless and fast, a single line repeated until it sounded like a prayer. He opened his door. The hallway lights flickered one by one, and from the far end came a small, polite voice. 'Come play,' it said. Dan did not move. Neither did the voice.

End of Chapter 1
