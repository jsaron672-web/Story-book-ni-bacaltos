The Hobbit

by J.R.R. Tolkien

Fantasy / Adventure

Synopsis

Bilbo Baggins joins a group of dwarves on a quest to reclaim their homeland and treasure from a dragon.

Chapter 1: An Unexpected Knock

Pip Underhill had a tidy home, a tidy garden and a very tidy life. So when a dozen loud travelers invaded his pantry, he fainted, quietly, into a basket of laundry.

'We need a fourteenth,' said the oldest of them, pointing a spoon. 'Someone small. Someone who won't be noticed.' Pip woke up holding a map and a strong desire to say no.

By morning the dwarves had eaten every biscuit in Pip's pantry and drawn a very large plan on his tablecloth. Their leader, a gruff old fellow named Thorrin, explained that a dragon slept on their treasure far away in the Lonely Mountain.

'And why do you need me?' Pip asked weakly. 'Because the dragon will smell a dwarf from a mile off,' Thorrin said. 'But a hobbit? He will not even know what you are.' Pip took a long look at his comfortable armchair, and a longer look at the open door.

Pip packed in a panic: two handkerchiefs, a loaf of seed cake, his grandfather's brass pocket-watch. He ran out into the rain with his door wide open and chased the company down the muddy road.

They were already at the crossroads. Thorrin tossed him a cloak that smelled of smoke and pine. 'Keep up,' he growled, 'and do not look back.' Pip looked back once. His little window glowed warm and tidy in the dark. Then he turned, took a breath and followed the dwarves into the wild.
