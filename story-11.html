The Great Gatsby

by F. Scott Fitzgerald

Classic / Tragedy

Synopsis

A mysterious millionaire pursues a lost romance while the story examines wealth, ambition, and the American Dream.

Chapter 1: Lights Across the Bay

Every Saturday the mansion next door blazed with music and champagne, and every Saturday its owner stood alone on the lawn. Nick watched him stretch one hand toward a green light on the water.

'Come to my party,' the man said at last, smiling like someone who had practiced. 'I'd like you to meet everyone. Everyone except the one I'm waiting for.'

The first party Nick attended was louder than a parade. Women in sequins laughed on the stairs and a band played on the roof. Nobody seemed to know who had invited them, and nobody seemed to care.

At last the host stepped forward and shook his hand. 'Old sport,' he said warmly. His smile was the kind you get four or five times in your life. Then he vanished, and Nick realized he had not heard the man's real name.

Nick wandered through the party until he found a library lined with real books, uncut pages and all. A woman fanning herself on a sofa laughed, 'He bought it by the shelf, you know.' Nick did not know whether she meant it as an insult or a compliment.

Near midnight, fireworks lit the bay. The host stood alone on the lawn, one hand stretched across the water toward a green light that blinked at the end of a distant dock. 'Come to lunch tomorrow,' he said without turning. 'There is someone you must meet.' Nick agreed before he understood why.

End of Chapter 1
