Dune

Dune

by Frank Herbert

Science Fiction / Political Adventure

Synopsis

A young nobleman becomes involved in a struggle over a desert planet whose rare resource is essential to the universe.

Chapter 1: The Desert Planet

The ship lowered through yellow clouds, and Paul saw only dunes in every direction. His father said the planet was a gift. His mother said it was a trap.

'Whoever controls the spice controls the stars,' a guide murmured. Far below, something enormous moved beneath the sand and the ground shivered like a drum.

Their home on the new planet was a stone fortress. Paul's mother taught him to listen to the wind, and his father taught him to listen to people. The Fremen, the tall, silent people of the desert, watched the newcomers from the ridge.

'They wear suits that save every drop of water,' a guard whispered. 'You do not spit here. You do not cry here either.' Paul looked at the horizon. 'Then I will learn,' he said, 'before the desert decides to teach me.'

That night, a sandstorm swallowed the fortress. In the howling dark, Paul saw a lone figure on the ridge, a woman in a dusty cloak who raised two fingers in a sign. 'She asks if you are friend,' the guide murmured. Paul raised his hand and, after a long moment, she nodded and vanished.

Below the fort, a deep drum-like thump rolled through the stone, slow and patient. His mother laid a hand on his shoulder. 'Whatever they tell you about this place,' she said softly, 'listen twice.' Paul stared at the empty ridge and wondered how many eyes had watched him arrive.

End of Chapter 1
